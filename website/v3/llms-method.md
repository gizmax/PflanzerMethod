## Pflanzer Method

Pflanzer Method is an open source (MIT) method and command-line tool for AI development in
companies. Representatives of all six steps of the path (business, product, design,
development, security and operations) work in shared sessions from day one. AI builds three
running variants in a three-hour Build, the team decides Go, Iterate or Kill on running code,
and the winner goes to development as a pull request with sign-offs. Created by Tomáš
Pflanzer (Wiseguys). Claim: "Everyone at the table. Code in production."

- [Pflanzer Method (English)](https://pflanzer.cz/method/en/): overview, path, outputs, FAQ.
- [Pflanzer Method (Czech)](https://pflanzer.cz/method/): the same page in Czech. Also German, Spanish, French, Italian and Polish under https://pflanzer.cz/method/<lang>/ (de, es, fr, it, pl).
- [Source code and docs](https://github.com/gizmax/PflanzerMethod): MIT licensed, version 0.6.
- [Comparison of 13 AI development methods](https://github.com/gizmax/PflanzerMethod/blob/main/docs/research/srovnani-metod-2026.md): research document (Czech).

Key facts: Kickoff 15 min, Build 3 h, feedback round 3-5 days, decision 60-90 min, Ship 1-2 days.
Ship quality threshold 80 of 100 for a pilot, 85 for production. 955 automated tests. 28 trial
runs on fictional projects; no results from real companies yet. Not intended for payments,
AI Act high-risk or DORA systems without a governance layer, native mobile apps, or projects
with strong external dependencies that cannot be simulated.
